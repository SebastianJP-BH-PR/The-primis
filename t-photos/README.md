# Team history

## PRNRO 2023

In our first year of competition, we participated in the Puerto Rico National Robot Olympiad, where we aimed to qualify for the World Championship in Panama. Even though we did not win, due to the impressive design of our robot, we were considered for participation in the RoboSports category.

## The Last RoboSports Competition

Due to changes in our circumstances, as all of the original members had graduated, Carlos was the last to participate in a PRNRO in 2025, accompanied by two "rookies." This was where the cycle of learning guided by this category came to an end. With an innovative design called "El Bloque," we closed the chapter that marked our era of dominance in the category and ultimately led us to participate in Future Engineers.
