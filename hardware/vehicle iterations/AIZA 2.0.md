---

 The first version of AIZA to have a working and installed [ASMS](../../hardware\mechanical\Pneumatics\ASMS\README.md) combined with the [eCVT V5](../../hardware\mechanical\eCVT\eCVT_5.0.md). Rather than having a flimsy lego chassis, the chassis was fully modeled and 3d printed, and we bolted the regulator and ASMS to it. Also had a Ackerman style steering system rather than a rack and pinion, and we placed the airtanks over the [Cytron Motion 2350 Pro](<../../hardware\electrical\Cytron Motion 2350 Pro\CYTRONREADME.md>).
